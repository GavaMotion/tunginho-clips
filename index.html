Tunginho Clips

A personal desktop tool

Tunginho Clips is a small app that runs on my own computer. I use it to edit short vertical clips (captions, cuts, sound effects) and then send the finished videos to my own TikTok account as drafts.

How it works

  1. I edit a short video on my computer.
  2. I sign in with TikTok once to give the app permission to upload to my account.
  3. The app uploads the video to my TikTok inbox as a draft.
  4. I open TikTok, review the draft, add the caption and decide whether to post it.

Nothing is posted automatically. Every video is reviewed and published by me inside the TikTok app.

Who can use it

The app is for my personal use only. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts of its own.
