Privacy Policy

Tunginho Clips · Last updated October 13, 2026

Summary

Tunginho Clips runs on your own computer. It has no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not sell or share your data.

What the app accesses

  • Your TikTok login token. When you connect TikTok, TikTok gives the app an access token with the user.info.basic and video.upload permissions. The token is stored only in a file on your computer.
  • Your TikTok display name, shown in the app so you can see which account is connected.
  • The videos you choose to upload. They are sent directly from your computer to TikTok.

What the app does not do

  • It does not read your followers, messages, likes, or any other TikTok data.
  • It does not post publicly on its own. Uploads arrive as drafts that you review and publish in TikTok.
  • It does not send your data to the app's author or to any third party other than TikTok itself.

Data retention and deletion

The token stays on your computer until you disconnect TikTok in the app, which deletes it. You can also revoke access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

TikTok's own policy

Videos you upload are handled by TikTok under the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and should only be used by people old enough to use TikTok.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top shows the latest version.
